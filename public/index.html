Our signature spread

The anatomy of a banana-leaf thali.

வாழை இலை சாப்பாடு

Eleven components, laid out the way they have been served in Tamil homes for generations — sweet first, rice at the centre, pickle and pappadam to the right. This is the spread we bring to weddings, house functions and corporate lunches.
